\block{1. Introduction \& Contributions}{
  \textbf{No-Limit Texas Hold'em (NLHE)} presents a massive AI challenge due to imperfect information, stochastic dynamics, continuous bet sizing, and sparse delayed rewards.

  We train autonomous agents via \textbf{self-play reinforcement learning}, where a shared policy improves by competing against itself.

  \textbf{Key Contributions:}
  \begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5em, itemsep=2pt]
    \item Custom multi-agent environment (2--10 players, cash-game dynamics).
    \item PPO implementation with strict \textbf{action masking} for legal moves.
    \item Interactive evaluation tools (GUI \& CLI) for real-time play.
  \end{itemize}
}

\block{3. Method: PPO with Action Masking}{
  We use \textbf{Proximal Policy Optimization (PPO)} within the Ray RLlib framework, employing a single shared policy (\textit{self-play}).

  \textbf{Action Masking:} Illegal actions are completely suppressed by setting their logits to $-10^{38}$ before the softmax layer, guaranteeing $\pi(a|s)=0$ for invalid moves:
  $$
    \ell_i'=\begin{cases}\ell_i&\text{if action }i\text{ is legal}\\-10^{38}&\text{otherwise}\end{cases}
  $$

  \textbf{Key Hyperparameters:}
  \vspace{0.3em}
  \begin{center}
  \renewcommand{\arraystretch}{1.2}
  \begin{tabular}{@{}ll@{}}
    \toprule
    \textbf{Hyperparameter} & \textbf{Value} \\
    \midrule
    Learning Rate & $3 \times 10^{-4}$ \\
    Discount $\gamma$ & $1.0$ (episodic) \\
    GAE $\lambda$ & $0.95$ \\
    Clip parameter $\epsilon$ & $0.2$ \\
    Entropy coefficient & $0.02$ \\
    Train batch size & $4096$ \\
    \bottomrule
  \end{tabular}
  \end{center}
}

  \begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5em, itemsep=3pt]
    \item \textbf{Shared trunk}: 2-layer MLP (d $\to$ 256 $\to$ 256) with ReLU

    \item \textbf{Critic head}: Linear(256, 128) $\to$ ReLU $\to$ Linear(128, 1)
  \end{itemize}

\block{8. Implementation \& Future Work}{
  \textbf{Tech Stack:} Ray RLlib 2.54 (RLModule API), PyTorch 2.10, Custom Gymnasium \texttt{MultiAgentEnv}, Treys library for hand evaluation.

  \textbf{Future Directions:}
  \begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5em, itemsep=3pt]
    \item Scale to 6--10 player tables and longer training horizons.
    \item Implement \textbf{population-based training} to avoid strategy collapse.
    \item Add \textbf{opponent modelling} and continuous bet sizing.
  \end{itemize}
}
